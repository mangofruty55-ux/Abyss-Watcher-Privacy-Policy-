Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

1. Overview

Abyss Watcher ("we", "us", "the bot") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

By using Abyss Watcher, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

2. Data We Collect

Abyss Watcher only collects data that is necessary to provide its features. The following data is stored in our database:

Data Type What We Store Purpose
Discord User ID Your unique Discord ID number Identifying your economy profile
Discord Server ID The server's unique ID number Separating per-server economies
Economy Data Balance, items, gear, stats, quests Core bot functionality
Vote History Last vote timestamp, vote streak Vote reward system
Command Usage Command counter per user Analytics and rank tracking
🔒 We do NOT collect: message content, usernames, email addresses, IP addresses, or any personal information beyond what Discord provides via its bot API.

3. How We Use Your Data

All collected data is used exclusively to provide Abyss Watcher's features:

  • Storing and retrieving your in-game economy progress
  • Processing commands and returning appropriate responses
  • Tracking quests, cooldowns, and vote rewards
  • Maintaining server-wide settings and leaderboards

We do not sell, rent, share, or monetize your data in any way. Your data is never used for advertising or shared with third parties.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in a MongoDB database hosted on secure cloud infrastructure. We use industry-standard security practices to protect your data including:

  • Encrypted database connections (TLS/SSL)
  • Access restricted to bot infrastructure only
  • No sensitive personal data is stored
  • Regular security reviews

However, no system is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted over the internet.

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as you or your server use Abyss Watcher. Specifically:

  • Server data is retained while the bot remains in your server
  • User data is retained unless you request deletion
  • Data may be automatically purged after extended periods of inactivity (12+ months)

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data at any time. To delete your data:

  • Join our support server and open a data deletion request
  • Your economy profile (all items, balance, progress) will be permanently deleted
  • This action is irreversible

Server owners can also request deletion of all server-related data by removing the bot and contacting support.

📬 To request data deletion, contact us via our support server: discord.gg/abysswatcher

7. Third-Party Services

Abyss Watcher interacts with the following third-party services:

  • Discord — Bot platform. Subject to Discord's Privacy Policy
  • top.gg — Bot listing and vote tracking. Subject to top.gg's Privacy Policy
  • MongoDB Atlas — Database hosting. Subject to MongoDB's Privacy Policy
  • Railway — Bot hosting infrastructure. Subject to Railway's Privacy Policy

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services.

8. Children's Privacy

Abyss Watcher is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the bot, please contact us so we can delete their data.

Use of Abyss Watcher is subject to Discord's minimum age requirements.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Abyss Watcher after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us through our official support server.

🌑 Support Server: discord.gg/abysswatcher

Your privacy matters to us. We only collect what we need to make the bot work.
